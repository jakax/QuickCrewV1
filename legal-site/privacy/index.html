This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME OF QUICKCREW] ("QuickCrew", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information through the QuickCrew mobile application and related services (together, the "App"). QuickCrew is a two-sided marketplace connecting Workers, who apply for and complete shifts, with Employers, who post and manage shifts. This policy applies to both types of users.

We are based in New Zealand and this Policy is written to comply with the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020, as well as the privacy requirements of the Apple App Store and Google Play Store.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on whether you use the App as a Worker or an Employer.

1.1 Account information (all users)

  • Email address and password — used to create and authenticate your account (via Firebase Authentication). Passwords are never stored in plain text.
  • Full name and phone number.

1.2 Worker profile information

To verify identity, eligibility to work, and safety before a Worker can be matched to shifts, we collect:

  • Preferred name, date of birth, gender, and nationality.
  • Home address (street, suburb, city, postcode).
  • Identity and right-to-work documents: passport number, passport expiry date, passport issuing country, and right-to-work / visa status in New Zealand (including visa expiry date where applicable).
  • Uploaded identity document (e.g. passport, NZ driver licence, or other valid NZ ID).
  • Uploaded visa document, where a Worker's right-to-work status requires one.
  • Resume/CV, uploaded as a document.
  • Profile photo.
  • Work references: name, company, role, phone number, email address, and notes about each referee. This is personal information about a third party that the Worker provides to us.
  • Emergency contact details: name, phone number, and relationship. This is also personal information about a third party that the Worker provides to us.
  • Criminal record check consent and, where a check is performed, [NAME OF BACKGROUND CHECK PROVIDER — confirm with QuickCrew if applicable].
  • A short "about" description written by the Worker.
  • Acceptance of Terms and Conditions and criminal check consent (recorded as timestamps/flags).

1.3 Employer / organization information

  • Organization name and description.
  • Role/member type within the organization and approval status.
  • Shift details posted by the Employer (title, location, description, date/time, hourly rate).

1.4 Work activity data

  • Shift applications, assignments, cancellations, and cancellation timing (including late-cancellation records).
  • Clock-in and clock-out timestamps for completed shifts.

1.5 Usage and diagnostic data

  • Usage data collected via Firebase Analytics (e.g. app interactions, feature usage), where enabled.
  • Diagnostic data collected via Firebase Crashlytics to help us identify and fix crashes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account and verify your identity.
  • To verify a Worker's eligibility to work in New Zealand.
  • To match Workers with relevant shifts and allow Employers to review applicants.
  • To manage shift applications, assignments, clock-in/out, and cancellations.
  • To contact a Worker's emergency contact only if genuinely necessary for the Worker's safety during a shift.
  • To run criminal background checks where required, with the Worker's consent.
  • To review and approve Worker and Employer profiles before they are activated on the platform.
  • To maintain the security, integrity, and stability of the App, including crash diagnostics.
  • To comply with our legal obligations and enforce our Terms and Conditions.

We do not use identity documents, passport information, or visa documents for any purpose other than verifying identity and right to work. We do not sell personal information to third parties.
