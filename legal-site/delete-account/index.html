QuickCrew — Account & Data Deletion

This page explains how you can request deletion of your QuickCrew account and the personal data associated with it, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

How to request deletion

Option 1 — In the app (self-service, instant):

  1. Open the QuickCrew app and log in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to the bottom of the screen.
  4. Tap Delete account.
  5. Confirm when prompted.

Your account and associated data are deleted immediately once confirmed — there is no waiting period.

Option 2 — By email (if you can't access the app):

Send a deletion request to info@quickcrew.co from the email address associated with your QuickCrew account, stating that you want your account and data deleted. We will process the request within a reasonable time.

Deleting specific data without deleting your account

You don't need to delete your entire account to remove specific pieces of information. Currently, QuickCrew lets you delete the following data on your own, at any time, while keeping your account active:

  • References: in the app, go to Profile, find the reference you want to remove, and tap Remove. It is deleted immediately.

For any other data associated with your profile (photo, CV, ID document, visa document, or personal details), you can update or replace it at any time from the Profile screen. If you'd like any of it permanently removed without replacing it, email info@quickcrew.co from your account's email address specifying what you'd like deleted, and we will action the request within a reasonable time. No additional retention period applies — once deleted, data is not kept.

What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, the following is permanently removed:

  • Your profile information (name, date of birth, nationality, phone number, address, emergency contact, references)
  • Uploaded documents (profile photo, CV, ID document, visa document)
  • Your login credentials (your account is removed from our authentication system)
  • For workers: your shift applications, shift assignments, and clock-in/clock-out records

What data may be retained

If your account is associated with an employer organization, that organization's own shift and job posting records are not deleted when you delete your personal account — this data belongs to the organization and may be shared with other members of that organization, independently of your individual account.

Beyond that, we do not apply any additional retention period: deletion of your personal account data is immediate and permanent.
